# harness-triage

Classify no-ceremony, QUICK, LIGHT, or FULL from irreversibility, sensitivity,
ambiguity, blast radius, and external effects.

- QUICK gets the narrowest adequate verification.
- LIGHT needs an observable outcome and TDD.
- FULL needs approved design, plan, adversarial review, and completion audit.
- Easy mechanical work may use Luna/low; focused work Terra/medium; security and hard work Sol/high.

Unknown or sensitive work never routes down. Apply
`.codex/skills/harness-delivery/references/delivery-contract.md` and
`.codex/skills/harness-rules/references/governance-contract.md` before choosing ceremony.
