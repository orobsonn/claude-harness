# harness-discovery

Keep questions product-facing and one at a time. Surface tradeoffs and success
criteria, not low-level implementation choices.

- Separate facts, assumptions, constraints, and unknowns.
- Turn a desired feature into observable user journeys and acceptance criteria.
- Avoid choosing an architecture before validating the product decision.
- Before closing a technical design, request an adversarial attack of its risks.

Use `.codex/skills/harness-delivery/references/delivery-contract.md` for the
entry tier and `.codex/skills/harness-rules/references/governance-contract.md` for adversarial scope.
